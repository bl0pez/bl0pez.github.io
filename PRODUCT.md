## Platform

web

## Stack

Astro (static site), deployed on Cloudflare. Chosen by the user.

## Users

Owners and managers of small and medium local businesses in Chile (restaurants, clinics, gyms, salons, workshops, shops). They arrive from a short cold email that pointed out one concrete problem with their current website, or from a Google search. Most read on a phone, between tasks. They are not technical: they want to know what problem gets solved, see real examples, and contact a real person quickly.

## Product Purpose

Personal portfolio and service site for Bryan López, an independent developer based in Arica, Chile. It must turn a curious business owner into a WhatsApp message or email. Success = a contact started.

## Positioning

One person who builds the whole thing end to end: websites, web and mobile apps, and n8n automations, with real production systems behind him (a gym management platform, a multi-vendor marketplace backend with payments and logistics, a loyalty SaaS). Direct contact with the developer, no agency layers.

## Product Principles

1. Show the problem solved, then the tech. Owners buy outcomes.
2. Every claim must be verifiable by a link.
3. Contact is always one tap away, especially on mobile.
4. The site must score excellently on mobile speed; it is part of the pitch.
5. Honest scope: state each role as the user's CV states it.

# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Astro (static site), deployed on Cloudflare. Chosen by the user.

## Users

Spanish-speaking people who saw one of Bryan's shorts (TikTok, Reels, YouTube Shorts) and want to make the same thing: creators, students and curious beginners. Most arrive on a phone from a link in a video or a DM. They are not programmers: they need what to install, in what order, and a prompt they can paste.

## Product Purpose

A tutorials hub: guides, quick tips and copy-ready prompts for creating videos, games (Roblox) and websites with AI tools such as Claude Code. Success = a visitor finishes a guide (copies the prompt) or joins the Discord.

## Positioning

Real workflows from someone who publishes with them every week, simplified for beginners. Free, in Spanish, with a Discord for help.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Content: tutorials grouped by topic (Videos con IA, Roblox, Webs y automatizaciones, Herramientas de IA), quick tips, Discord community (https://discord.gg/HxD6seBkKt). The former business portfolio (services, cases, AI demos) was removed in Nov 2026.

Language: Spanish (Chile, neutral, "tú"/"ustedes" for business owners; avoid Argentine voseo).
Domains: blopezdev.com now; blopez.cl planned as primary.
